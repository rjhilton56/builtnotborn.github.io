FAQ QUESTION: When does FORGE launch?
FAQ ANSWER: FORGE is pre-launch. It's in final device testing ahead of App Store review, launching on iOS first. Join early access on the community page and you'll hear the moment it lands.
FAQ QUESTION: Is FORGE free?
FAQ ANSWER: FORGE will be free to try when it launches on iOS. The full experience will be £7.99 per month; annual pricing will be announced at launch. The physical FORGE Journal is a separate, optional product at £14.99.

FAQ QUESTION: Is FORGE coming to Android?
FAQ ANSWER: FORGE launches on iOS first. Android is planned. Join the Built Not Born list and you'll hear when it lands.
