FORGE is a self-development app for men built around a daily check-in. It writes a personal AI reflection from your own words, generates meditations for the day you actually had, and includes breathwork, visualisation, a guided morning ritual and long-term pattern coaching.

Write your daily check-in in the physical FORGE journal, then scan the page with the app. FORGE reads your handwriting and your coach responds to what you actually wrote, so the ritual of pen on paper feeds the intelligence of the app.
