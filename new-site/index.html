If You Want Both

The bridge is optional.
The philosophy isn't.

Some men want pen and paper. Some want the coach. Some choose to bridge the two: write by hand in the journal, scan the page, then get AI coaching from your own handwriting. That's the best of both worlds, not the "correct" way to use FORGE.

i

Write by hand

Your daily check-in goes into the FORGE Journal. Pen on paper, the oldest tool for self-mastery.

ii

Scan the page

Open the app and scan what you wrote. FORGE reads your handwriting: your wins, your pressure, your patterns.

iii

Get coached back

Your coach answers from your own words. The ritual of paper, the intelligence of something that misses nothing.

However you write, you enter the same reflection system.
