FORGE Privacy Policy

Built Not Born. Contact: admin@builtnotborn.uk

Last updated · October 2026

The short version

  • Your journal is yours. It is never sold and never shared with advertisers. Our AI provider, Anthropic, does not train its models on it.
  • What you write is stored in your FORGE account. It is sent to our AI provider so FORGE can write your reflections.
  • Some of what you write may reveal information about your mental or physical health. FORGE only processes it with your explicit consent and only to run the app for you.
  • You can delete any entry or your whole account in the app at any time.

Who we are

FORGE is an iOS journaling and self-development app. Joe Hilton, a sole trader trading as Built Not Born in the United Kingdom is the data controller for your personal data. You can contact us at admin@builtnotborn.uk.

What FORGE collects

Data
What it includes and why
Account
Your email address, to create and secure your account. Your password is encrypted by our login provider and never seen by us.
Profile
First name, who you are, who you're becoming, vision, motivation, routines, core values, custom habit and goals. Used to personalise FORGE.
Daily check-ins
Feelings, energy, stress, water, habits, self-care, gratitude, your win, your human moment, who you connected with, your written reflection and tomorrow's priority. Your journal and the basis of your daily reflection.
FORGE Space
Private context you choose to write for FORGE. Used to shape reflections, coaching and guided sessions while it is switched on.
Tool entries
Weekly reviews, monthly milestones, 90-day reflections, Wheel of Life scores, values check-ins, Strikes writing, perspective shifts, grounding, named emotions and your breathwork and meditation sessions. Used to run those tools and show your history.
AI responses
Reflections, coaching insights and follow-up questions written for you, so you can read them again.
Journal page photos
A photo of your handwritten journal when you use journal scan. Used to read the page into your check-in. FORGE does not store the photo.
Steps
Your daily step count from Apple Health, only with your permission. Shown in the app and stored with your check-in. Never sent to any AI provider.
Subscription
Whether you subscribe, which plan and when it renews or ends, so subscribers get access. Apple takes payment and we never see your card details.
Usage events
Actions such as a screen opened or a check-in saved, with the app version and device type. Never your journal text. Used to fix problems and improve FORGE.
Safety flags
The risk level and time when an entry shows signs of crisis. Never the text itself.
Notification settings
Your reminder time, whether reminders are on and whether to show your priority. Reminders are created on your phone.
Consent
When you gave consent and to which version of this policy.

FORGE does not collect your location, contacts, browsing history, microphone audio or advertising identifiers. It has no advertising and no third-party trackers.

How FORGE uses AI

Reflections, coaching, journal scans and guided session scripts are written by Anthropic's Claude AI through Anthropic's API. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models and keeps it only for a limited period under its commercial terms. What each feature sends:

Daily reflection. That day's check-in, your first name, your profile, your goals, your last seven days of entries and your FORGE Space content.

FORGE Insight. Up to 90 days of entries, your recent weekly reviews, your name and profile, your streak and your FORGE Space content. This is what lets Insight comment on patterns across months.

Meditation and visualisation. A duration, a session type, summary values for your energy, stress and mood and your FORGE Space content. The script that comes back is turned into audio by Speechify, which receives the script only. If Speechify is unavailable, async.com does this instead and also receives the script only.

Strikes. The text of the writing session and the prompt used.

Journal page scanning. The photograph of the page only.

Your email address and your step count are never sent to Anthropic, Speechify or async.com. AI can be wrong. FORGE is not therapy or medical advice.

Safety checks

When you write in FORGE, the server checks the text for signs that you may be in serious distress or at risk of harm, using keyword checks and the AI.

If it finds them, FORGE shows support information such as Samaritans instead of an ordinary reply. It records the risk level and the time only, never what you wrote. That record is deleted with your account.

FORGE is not a crisis service and does not monitor you in real time. If you are struggling, call 999 or Samaritans on 116 123.

Our legal basis

  • Contract. To provide the FORGE service you sign up for (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).
  • Explicit consent. For any health information in what you write (Article 9(2)(a)). FORGE asks for this before you start. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting your account or emailing us.
  • Legitimate interests. To keep FORGE secure, prevent misuse, improve the app from usage events and produce anonymised statistics (Article 6(1)(f)).

How long we keep it

  • Your data is kept while you have an account.
  • Deleting an entry removes it straight away.
  • Deleting your account (Profile, then Delete Account) removes your data from FORGE's database, your login, your RevenueCat record and the FORGE data saved on your phone. It cannot be undone.
  • Weekly database backups are kept for up to 8 weeks, then deleted. A deleted account can remain in a backup until it expires. Backups are only ever used to recover from a fault.
  • Usage events are deleted with your account. FORGE keeps an anonymous count of deleted accounts showing only the week you signed up.

If deletion in the app fails, email admin@builtnotborn.uk and it will be done within 30 days.

Anonymised statistics

We may combine data from many members into anonymised statistics, such as how many members check in each week, how long people keep a streak or how often guided sessions are used. These figures never include anything you wrote and cannot be used to identify you.

We use them to improve FORGE and may publish them, use them in our marketing or share them with partners. Anything that could identify you is never shared without your explicit consent.

If Built Not Born changes hands

If Built Not Born or FORGE is sold, merged or transferred to another organisation, your data may move to the new owner so the service can continue. They would have to protect it under this policy. We would tell you before anything changed.

On your phone

FORGE saves some things on your phone, such as an unfinished check-in and today's coaching insight. They are cleared when you sign out or delete your account. Reminders are created on your phone. Your priority only appears on the lock screen if you switch that on in Profile.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its use, receive a copy to move elsewhere and withdraw consent. Most of this you can do in the app. For a copy of your data or anything else, email admin@builtnotborn.uk. We reply within one month.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Security

Your records can only be read by your own signed-in account. All connections are encrypted. The FORGE server's own credentials are held on the server only, never in the app. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the ICO where the law requires.

Age

FORGE is for adults aged 18 and over. If we learn that someone under 18 has an account, we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes. Significant changes are shown in the app. If FORGE ever needs to use your data in a new way that needs consent, it will ask again first.
