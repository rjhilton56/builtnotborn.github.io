Stage One · The Check-In

Six steps.
Two minutes.

Mood, actions, your win, a written reflection, tomorrow's priority. The daily check-in is the raw material everything else is forged from. It takes less time than making coffee.

i

ii

iii

Private by design

Stage Two · AI Reflection & Insight

Feedback built
from your life.
Not a template.

FORGE reads the check-in you actually wrote. It looks at today in the context of what you have logged before, then answers from that history. You can reply. Your reply is saved against that day.

Inside The App

Forged in ember and gold.

Asked honestly.
Answered the same way.

An AI journal app for men built around a daily check-in. FORGE writes a reflection from your own words, then draws on your history as the record grows. It also includes guided breathwork, generated meditation and visualisation, timed writing sprints, a morning ritual and a knowledge library.

FORGE is pre-launch. It's in final device testing ahead of App Store review, launching on iOS first. Join early access on the community page and you'll hear the moment it lands.

There is no programme to finish. FORGE is built for ongoing use. Keep using it for as long as it earns its place. Only the paper journal runs to 90 days.

FORGE will be free to try when it launches on iOS. After that it is £7.99 per month for everything in the app. One tier. The physical FORGE Journal is a separate product at £14.99 on Amazon.
